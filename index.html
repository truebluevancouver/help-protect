WHY THIS EXISTS

A simple fund with one purpose: safety.

Daniel takes part in public events and community activities. At times, that can mean dealing with hostility, confrontational comments, or situations where additional planning is sensible.

The goal is straightforward: help cover reasonable professional security and safety-related costs without publishing sensitive details.
