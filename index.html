THE PURPOSE

Public participation deserves responsible protection.

The fund is designed around one priority: helping Daniel participate in public life more safely, while keeping sensitive security details private.

THE MISSION

Protection without the noise.

Public events and community work can bring difficult or hostile interactions. When additional protection is appropriate, cost should not be the reason sensible safety measures are out of reach.

Defend Daniel exists to help with legitimate security and safety expenses while respecting privacy and keeping the focus where it belongs: safer participation.
